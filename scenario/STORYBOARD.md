# Nile Vision Electronics S.A.E. - integrated demo storyboard

Company (invented): **Nile Vision Electronics S.A.E. (NVE)**, 10th of Ramadan City. Assembles LED/QLED TVs from imported open cells.
Window: history **2026-07-01 to 2026-09-28**, demo "today" **2026-09-29 (Tuesday)**. Opening balance sheet **2026-06-30**.
Source of every number: `scenario/book.json` (generated by `scenario/generate-book.mjs`, seed 20260929). Industry ground truth:
`research/TV_INDUSTRY_REFERENCE.md` (provenance tags [S#] = cited, [E] = engineering estimate; the same tags appear in the `source` fields of the book).

Systems: **Mizan** (ERP / accounting), **GMES** (MES + planning / MRP), **HR-System**, **Space Planner** (3D layout). Screen names below are the
target screens of the master plan (`plan/10..40`); GMES screen codes are the real ones (PLN2010 ...).

## 0. One page for the CEO

**What you will see.** One company, one company id, four systems that share facts instead of retyping them. A retailer order entered in
Mizan reaches the factory floor in GMES, turns into purchase orders in Mizan, crew needs in HR, serial-numbered TVs, a delivery, an
e-invoice and a bank collection - and every number reconciles across the four systems.

**The company.** ⟦co_sets_year⟧ sets planned for 2026, six models (43" to 65", UHD and QLED), one SMT line, one power-board THT cell, two final
assembly lines (FA-1 43-55", FA-2 55-65"), ⟦hc_perm⟧ permanent people, 6 domestic customers, 2 export distributors, 16 material suppliers.
Sales for the 3 months: **EGP ⟦rev_total_m⟧ M** (domestic ⟦rev_dom_m⟧ M, export ⟦rev_exp_m⟧ M); gross margin ⟦gm_pct⟧ %; ⟦sets_produced⟧ sets built.

**The story in five acts.**

| Act | When | What happens | Why a CEO cares |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Business as usual | Jul-Aug | Monthly S&OP, weekly MRP, steady production, imports, sales, collections, two month-end closes. Forecast is only ⟦fc_acc_pct⟧ % accurate, absenteeism 4.5 %, a supplier is late. | The baseline the KPIs are measured against. |
| B. "White Friday" mega order | 17 Aug - 28 Sep | Nile Mega Electronics orders **⟦mega_total⟧ TVs (EGP ⟦mega_value_m⟧ M ex VAT)** for three lots before 24 Sep. ATP shows only ⟦mega_atp_first⟧ available. Open cells need 70 days by sea, so ⟦air_panels⟧ are air-freighted (+EGP ⟦air_premium_m⟧ M landed cost), ⟦mega_deferred⟧ sets move to a 4th lot on 8 Oct. FA-2 runs a night shift for 4 weeks with 40 agency temps, trained and qualified before touching the line. | Shows the whole chain reacting to one order, and what it costs. |
| C. KSA export | 12 Aug - 10 Sep | Al-Waha Trading (Jeddah): ⟦exp_ksa_sets⟧ sets in 10 x 40HC, CIF Jeddah, LC at sight, zero-rated VAT, USD invoice and collection with an FX difference of EGP ⟦exp_fx_diff⟧. | Export margin and FX exposure. |
| D. Supplier quality incident | 6-15 Sep | An open-cell lot passes IQC but fails on the line. GMES traces every set built from the lot: ⟦inc_built⟧ built, ⟦inc_shipped⟧ already at a dealer. Recall, credit note, supplier claim. | Traceability is the difference between a 120-set recall and a 5,000-set recall. |
| E. September close | 28-30 Sep | Variances, PPV, landed cost, FX revaluation, payroll with overtime, night allowance and agency temps, bank reconciliation, KPI pack. | One reconciled set of numbers for the board. |

**KPIs at the end of the window (targets vs realistic actuals, see section 8).** OTIF domestic key accounts ⟦kpi_otif_actual⟧ % (target 95),
OEE FA ⟦kpi_oee_fa⟧ % (target 82), FPY final assembly ⟦kpi_fpy_fa⟧ % (target 98.0), DSO ⟦kpi_dso⟧ d, inventory turns ⟦kpi_turns⟧ (target 9),
forecast accuracy ⟦fc_acc_pct⟧ % (target 75). The mega order costs a clear, explainable dip in OTIF for one customer and a temporary OEE dip on the night shift.

**Three decisions the demo asks the audience to make:** (1) approve the exceptional S&OP for the mega order (17-18 Aug); (2) approve
air freight of open cells at a premium versus telling the customer a later date (19 Aug); (3) approve the night shift and the 40 agency temps (19 Aug).

## 1. Conventions used in the demo

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Codes | Company `NVE`, plant `EG-NV1`, lines `SMT-1`, `THT-1`, `FA-1`, `FA-2`; **station = `<line>-<operation>`** (e.g. `FA-2-OCM` = open-cell mounting on FA-2). Items `OC-55-SSD`, `NV-65U`, ... Sales orders `SO-2026-nnnnn`, purchase orders `PO-2026-nnnnn`, requisitions `PR-<run>-<n>` (GMES), work orders `WO-<yyyymmdd>-<line>-<shift>`, employees `E000001`... |
| Calendar | Working week Saturday-Thursday, **Friday rest**. Holidays in the window: 2026-07-23 (Revolution Day), 2026-08-26 (Mawlid, lunar date [E]). Banks work Sunday-Thursday. |
| Shifts | A 07:00-15:00, B 15:00-23:00, C 23:00-07:00 (C only on FA-2, production dates 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-24; an overnight shift belongs to the date it starts). 8 h span = 1 h rest + 30 min meeting/5S + 6.5 h net production. |
| Money | EGP functional; USD for imports/exports. Plan FX 51.00; daily rates in `meta.fx.daily` (window 50.4-51.4, drifting up). Import VAT 14 % recoverable, duty 2 % on components, ETA e-invoice on every invoice. |
| Quantities | Exact integers; the book never needs a rounded quantity. |

## 9. Presenter tips

- Start on the portal, show the four green lights and the **same company id** on all four.
- Follow one document across systems: `SO-2026-⟦mega_so_no⟧` (Mizan) -> `PLN2040` pegging (GMES) -> `PR-...` -> `PO-...` -> GR -> IQC -> work orders -> shipment -> delivery -> invoice -> PDC cheque.
- The drill-down every expert asks for: **serial number -> genealogy (open-cell lot, main-board serial) -> delivery -> customer** (arc D).
- If a number is challenged, open `book.json#checks`: every reconciliation lists its inputs and the expected value.
